## How it works

Understanding your own Google Business Profile performance is important, but seeing how it compares with nearby competitors provides the context needed to make better local SEO decisions. Rankerly's Competitor Tracking feature analyzes businesses competing in the same local market, allowing you to compare rankings, visibility, and overall search performance from a single location.

Instead of manually searching Google to see which businesses appear alongside yours, Rankerly brings competitor insights into one centralized dashboard. You can monitor how your business compares with local competitors, understand your relative visibility in search results, and identify businesses that consistently perform well for the keywords that matter most.

Each Google Business Profile location has its own competitor analysis, ensuring comparisons remain relevant to the local market. Since competitors and search results differ between cities and neighborhoods, tracking competitors on a per-location basis provides a more accurate picture of your local search performance and competitive position.

Whether you're evaluating your current rankings, monitoring visibility against nearby competitors, or looking for opportunities to strengthen your local presence, Competitor Tracking gives you the insights needed to make more informed optimization decisions. By combining competitor rankings, visibility comparisons, and local market analysis in one workspace, Rankerly helps you better understand where your business stands and where improvements can have the greatest impact.

## Explore your competitive landscape

### Compare local rankings

Competitor Tracking lets you compare your Google Business Profile with other businesses competing in the same local market. By viewing ranking positions side by side, you can better understand where your business stands for important local searches and identify competitors that consistently appear ahead of you.

### Measure local visibility

Ranking alone doesn't always tell the full story. Competitor Tracking also helps you understand how visible your business is compared with nearby competitors, giving you a broader view of your position within the local search landscape and helping you measure your overall presence.

### Analyze competitor performance

Understanding how competitors perform provides valuable context for your own optimization efforts. Compare ranking trends, visibility, and overall performance to see which businesses are leading your local market and where your Business Profile has opportunities to improve.

### Location-specific competitor insights

Every Google Business Profile location competes with a different set of businesses. That's why Competitor Tracking analyzes competitors individually for each location, ensuring the insights you receive are relevant to the specific market where that business operates.

### Discover optimization opportunities

Comparing your Business Profile with nearby competitors makes it easier to identify strengths and weaknesses. Whether competitors consistently rank higher or maintain greater visibility, these insights can help you prioritize improvements that may have the biggest impact on your local search performance.

### Make data-driven SEO decisions

Local SEO is more effective when it's based on real competitive data instead of assumptions. Competitor Tracking brings rankings, visibility, and competitor analysis together in one place, helping you evaluate your local market, measure your position against nearby businesses, and make more informed optimization decisions.

## How it works

Maintain a consistent Google Business Profile publishing schedule without needing to remember to post every day. Rankerly's Scheduled Posts feature lets you create your content in advance, choose the exact date and time it should be published, and automatically publish it to your Google Business Profile when the scheduled time arrives.

Instead of manually creating and publishing updates whenever you have time, you can plan promotions, announcements, events, offers, and general business updates ahead of schedule. This allows you to organize your content calendar, reduce last-minute work, and ensure your Business Profile always stays active with fresh, timely content.

Scheduled Posts is designed for businesses that want to maintain a consistent online presence while spending less time managing their Google Business Profile. By scheduling posts in advance, you can focus on running your business instead of worrying about remembering when your next update should go live. Whether you're planning seasonal campaigns, holiday promotions, product launches, or recurring announcements, scheduling helps you deliver content exactly when your audience is most likely to see it.

Planning your content ahead also gives you greater flexibility in your marketing strategy. Instead of reacting when it's time to publish another update, you can organize your upcoming posts, review your publishing calendar, and automate your posting schedule. This helps maintain consistency, improves efficiency, and ensures customers always see fresh, relevant content whenever they visit your Business Profile.

## A closer look

### Schedule posts for the perfect time

Instead of manually publishing every update, Scheduled Posts lets you decide exactly when each Google Business Profile post should go live. Simply create your content, choose a publish date and time, and Rankerly automatically publishes it for you. This gives you complete control over your posting schedule while removing the need to remember every upcoming update.

### Plan your content calendar

Successful local marketing relies on consistency, but finding time to create and publish posts every week isn't always easy. With Scheduled Posts, you can build your content calendar in advance by scheduling promotions, announcements, events, and business updates ahead of time. Planning your content this way makes it easier to stay organized and maintain a steady flow of fresh content throughout the month.

### Schedule promotions and special offers

Promotions are often tied to specific dates, making timing just as important as the content itself. Whether you're running a seasonal sale, limited-time offer, or holiday campaign, you can schedule promotional posts to publish automatically at exactly the right moment. This helps ensure customers see your offers when they're most relevant without requiring you to publish them manually.

### Keep customers informed with announcements

Business announcements such as new services, holiday hours, product launches, or company updates can all be prepared ahead of time. Instead of waiting until the day of the announcement, simply schedule your post and let Rankerly publish it automatically. This helps you communicate consistently while reducing last-minute work.

### Schedule event updates

If you're hosting upcoming events, workshops, or community activities, Scheduled Posts makes it easy to promote them in advance. Choose the ideal publication date so customers learn about your event at the right time, helping improve visibility and giving people more opportunity to attend.

### Automate your publishing workflow

Publishing posts manually every few days can quickly become another task on an already busy schedule. Scheduled Posts automates that process by publishing your content at the time you've selected. Instead of remembering when your next update is due, you can focus on running your business while maintaining an active and engaging Google Business Profile.

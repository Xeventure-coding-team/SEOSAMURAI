## How it works

Understanding where your business appears in local search results is an important part of improving your online visibility. Rankerly's Keyword Tracking feature lets you monitor how an individual Google Business Profile location ranks for the keywords that matter most to your business. Simply add the search terms you want to track, and Rankerly regularly checks their ranking positions so you can see how your local search performance changes over time.

Instead of manually searching Google to check where your Business Profile appears, Keyword Tracking automates the process and records previous ranking positions for every keyword you track. This makes it easy to see how rankings change over time, identify improvements or declines, and monitor your local search visibility without relying on manual searches that can produce inconsistent results.

Every business location has its own dedicated keyword list, allowing you to track rankings specifically for that location. Since local search results vary by location, monitoring keywords individually provides a much more accurate picture of how a particular Business Profile performs than relying on general searches or estimates.

Whether you're improving your Google Business Profile, working on your local SEO strategy, or simply monitoring important search terms, Keyword Tracking gives you clear visibility into your ranking performance. Previous ranking records help you measure SEO progress, evaluate optimization efforts, and better understand how your Business Profile's visibility changes over time.

## Explore your keyword performance

### Track the keywords that matter

Every business relies on different search terms to attract customers. Keyword Tracking allows you to build a custom list of keywords for each Google Business Profile location, giving you complete control over which searches you monitor. Instead of guessing where your business appears, you can track the keywords that are most relevant to your products, services, and local market.

### See how rankings change over time

Search rankings are constantly changing as competitors update their listings and search results evolve. Rankerly records each keyword's ranking over time, making it easy to see whether your Business Profile is moving up, dropping down, or maintaining a consistent position. This ongoing history helps you understand how your local visibility develops over the weeks and months.

### Review previous ranking positions

Looking at today's ranking only tells part of the story. Keyword Tracking keeps a history of previous ranking checks for every tracked keyword, allowing you to compare current positions with earlier results. This makes it easier to measure progress, identify long-term trends, and understand how your rankings have changed.

### Organize keywords for each location

Every Google Business Profile location has its own keyword list and ranking history. Since businesses often rank differently depending on their location, tracking keywords separately provides a much clearer picture of local search performance than combining results across multiple locations.

### Measure the impact of your SEO work

Updating your Business Profile, improving business information, collecting reviews, or optimizing your local SEO can all influence search rankings. Keyword Tracking helps you monitor whether these efforts are making a difference by showing how your keyword positions change over time, giving you a practical way to evaluate your optimization strategy.

### Make smarter local SEO decisions

Reliable ranking data helps you make informed decisions instead of relying on assumptions. By monitoring keyword performance regularly, you can identify opportunities to improve visibility, focus on keywords that matter most to your business, and better understand how customers are finding your Business Profile in local search.

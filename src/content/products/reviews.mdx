## How it works

Managing customer reviews becomes increasingly difficult as your business grows, especially when feedback is spread across multiple Google Business Profile locations. Rankerly centralizes review management by providing dedicated workspaces for responding to new reviews and analyzing review performance, helping your team stay organized while maintaining excellent customer engagement.

The main Reviews dashboard is designed to help your team focus on action. Instead of browsing through every review you've ever received, it displays reviews that are still awaiting a response across all connected locations. This allows you to quickly identify new customer feedback, prioritize unanswered reviews, generate AI-powered reply suggestions, and respond directly from one place without switching between individual Business Profiles.

Every business location also includes its own dedicated Reviews page, giving you access to the complete review history for that specific Google Business Profile. Browse every customer review, monitor rating trends, explore review analytics, understand customer sentiment, and evaluate how feedback changes over time. Having both an action-focused dashboard and a detailed location view makes it easier to manage day-to-day customer interactions while gaining valuable insights into your business's reputation.

Whether you're responding to today's newest reviews or analyzing months of customer feedback for a single location, Rankerly helps reduce manual work, improve response times, and ensure every customer review receives the attention it deserves. By combining centralized review management, AI-assisted replies, and location-specific analytics, businesses can build stronger customer relationships while maintaining a positive online reputation.

## Everything you need to manage reviews

### Stay on top of unanswered reviews

The main Reviews dashboard is designed to help your team focus on customer feedback that still needs attention. Instead of searching through every review across every location, Rankerly automatically surfaces reviews that haven't yet received a reply. This allows you to prioritize responses, stay organized, and ensure every customer receives timely engagement without unnecessary account switching.

### Respond with AI-powered assistance

Crafting thoughtful review responses can take valuable time, especially for businesses receiving reviews every day. Rankerly's AI-powered reply suggestions generate professional, context-aware responses based on the customer's feedback. You can review, customize, and publish replies directly from the dashboard, making it faster to respond while maintaining a consistent tone across every customer interaction.

### Explore complete review history

Beyond the centralized review inbox, every business location has its own dedicated Reviews page containing the complete history of customer feedback. Browse all reviews, revisit previous conversations, filter reviews when needed, and manage every interaction from a single location-specific workspace designed for long-term review management.

### Discover trends through review analytics

Customer reviews tell a much bigger story than individual ratings alone. Rankerly provides location-specific analytics that help you monitor average ratings, review volume, customer sentiment, response performance, and long-term trends. These insights help you understand how customers perceive your business and identify opportunities to improve service quality.

### Manage reviews across every location

Whether you're responsible for two locations or hundreds, keeping track of customer feedback manually can become overwhelming. Rankerly centralizes review management by bringing customer feedback from every connected Google Business Profile into one platform. Your team can monitor activity, respond to reviews, and access detailed location insights without constantly switching between accounts.

### Build stronger customer relationships

Responding to reviews consistently shows customers that their opinions matter. By combining centralized review management, AI-assisted replies, and detailed analytics, Rankerly helps businesses engage with customers more effectively, improve response rates, strengthen their online reputation, and turn customer feedback into meaningful business improvements.

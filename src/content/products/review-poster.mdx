## How it works

Collecting customer reviews shouldn't require explaining where customers need to go or sending links after every visit. Rankerly's Review Poster feature lets you generate professional review request posters with a built-in QR code that takes customers directly to your Google Business Profile review page. Simply display the poster where customers can easily see it, and they can leave a review by scanning the QR code with their phone.

Instead of manually asking customers to search for your business on Google before leaving a review, Review Poster removes unnecessary steps from the process. Customers simply scan the QR code, open your review page instantly, and share their experience within minutes. Making the review process easier helps encourage more customers to leave feedback while reducing the friction that often prevents reviews from being submitted.

Review Poster is ideal for businesses that regularly interact with customers in person, including restaurants, retail stores, clinics, salons, hotels, gyms, offices, and service providers. Whether displayed at a reception desk, checkout counter, waiting area, entrance, or meeting room, the poster serves as a simple reminder that encourages satisfied customers to share their experience while it's still fresh in their minds.

You can download and print your review poster for physical locations or share it digitally through email, messaging apps, invoices, or other customer communications. By making it quick and convenient for customers to access your Google review page, Review Poster helps increase review volume, strengthen your online reputation, and build greater trust with future customers.

## Make it easy for customers to leave reviews

### Generate your poster in seconds

Creating a review request poster shouldn't require design experience. Rankerly automatically generates a professional poster with your business information and a QR code that links directly to your Google Business Profile review page. Within seconds, you'll have a ready-to-use poster that can be displayed anywhere customers interact with your business.

### QR codes that take customers directly to your review page

The easier it is to leave a review, the more likely customers are to complete it. Every Review Poster includes a unique QR code that opens your Google Business Profile review form instantly. Customers simply scan the code with their smartphone and are taken directly to the page where they can share their experience without searching for your business.

### Perfect for any customer touchpoint

Review Posters can be placed wherever customers naturally spend time. Display them at reception desks, checkout counters, waiting rooms, restaurant tables, hotel lobbies, retail stores, offices, or service vehicles. Positioning the poster where customers finish their experience creates a simple reminder to leave feedback while their visit is still fresh in their minds.

### Print or share digitally

Review Posters aren't limited to physical locations. Download your poster for high-quality printing or share it digitally through email, invoices, SMS, WhatsApp, social media, or other customer communications. Whether customers visit in person or interact with your business online, they can quickly access your review page with a single scan.

### Encourage more customer feedback

Many satisfied customers are happy to leave a review but never do because the process feels inconvenient. By removing unnecessary steps and providing direct access to your review page, Review Poster makes it significantly easier for customers to share their feedback, helping you collect reviews more consistently over time.

### Build trust with every new review

Customer reviews influence purchasing decisions and strengthen your online reputation. Every additional review helps potential customers feel more confident about choosing your business. By making review collection simple and accessible, Review Poster helps you grow your review count, improve credibility, and build stronger trust with future customers.

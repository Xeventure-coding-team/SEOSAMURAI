## How it works

A strong local presence starts with a professional website. Rankerly's Websites feature allows you to create dedicated websites for your Google Business Profile locations, giving each website its own identity while keeping it connected to the location it represents.

Every website is linked to a single Google Business Profile location, ensuring customers always see information that's relevant to the business they're searching for. You can create as many websites as you need, with each website associated with one specific location, making it easy to manage websites across your entire business.

Instead of building location websites from scratch, Rankerly helps you quickly create professional pages that showcase business information, contact details, services, and other location-specific content. Each website is managed from one centralized platform, making updates and ongoing maintenance much simpler.

Whether you're creating a website for a new location, launching a dedicated landing page for a specific branch, or managing websites across multiple business locations, Rankerly provides an efficient way to build, organize, and maintain location-focused websites that support your local marketing efforts and online visibility.

## Build websites for every location

### Create a website in minutes

Getting a professional website online shouldn't require starting from scratch. Rankerly makes it easy to create a website for any Google Business Profile location, allowing you to launch location-specific websites quickly without the complexity of traditional website builders.

### Connect every website to a location

Every website is associated with a single Google Business Profile location, ensuring the content, contact details, and business information remain relevant to that specific branch. This keeps your local presence organized and provides customers with accurate information for the location they're visiting.

### Manage multiple websites

Whether you're operating a few locations or managing hundreds, Rankerly provides a centralized workspace for managing all of your websites. Instead of maintaining separate projects across different platforms, you can organize every location website from one dashboard.

### Showcase your business

Each website is designed to highlight the information customers care about most. Display business details, services, contact information, operating hours, maps, and other location-specific content to help visitors quickly learn more about your business.

### Support your local marketing

Dedicated location websites help strengthen your local marketing efforts by giving every business location its own online presence. They complement your Google Business Profile and provide customers with an additional place to discover your services and learn more about your business.

### Grow as your business grows

As you open new locations, creating additional websites is simple. Every new website can be linked to its own Google Business Profile location, making it easy to scale your online presence while keeping everything organized and easy to manage from a single platform.

## How it works

Traditional keyword tracking shows how your Google Business Profile ranks from a single search location, but local rankings can vary dramatically across a city or service area. Rankerly's Geo-Grid Scan feature helps you understand those differences by checking your rankings from multiple points within a defined geographic area and presenting the results in an easy-to-read visual grid.

Instead of relying on a single ranking position, Geo-Grid Scans simulate searches from dozens of different locations around your business. Each point in the grid represents a unique search location, allowing you to see exactly where your Business Profile performs well and where visibility begins to decline. This provides a far more complete picture of your local search presence than a single ranking alone.

Every scan is tied to a specific Google Business Profile location and keyword, making it easy to analyze local performance for individual locations. By comparing grid results over time, you can evaluate the impact of your local SEO efforts, identify areas where visibility is improving, and discover parts of your service area that may need additional optimization.

Whether you're managing one location or many, Geo-Grid Scans provide valuable insight into how customers across different neighborhoods are likely to find your business in Google Maps. The visual grid makes complex ranking data simple to understand, helping you make smarter decisions about your local SEO strategy.

## Explore your local visibility

### See rankings across your service area

A single ranking doesn't tell the whole story. Geo-Grid Scans check your Google Business Profile from multiple search locations, showing how your rankings change across different neighborhoods and areas. This provides a much clearer picture of your real local visibility.

### Visualize your coverage

Scan results are displayed as an interactive grid, making it easy to identify where your business performs well and where rankings begin to decline. Instead of interpreting spreadsheets of ranking data, you can quickly understand your coverage area at a glance.

### Analyze individual keywords

Every Geo-Grid Scan is performed for a specific keyword, allowing you to understand how your Business Profile performs for the searches that matter most to your business. Different keywords often produce different coverage patterns, helping you identify strengths and opportunities.

### Scan each business location independently

Every Google Business Profile location can have its own Geo-Grid Scans. This allows you to evaluate local visibility separately for each branch, office, or storefront, ensuring your insights accurately reflect the market surrounding that location.

### Compare previous scans

As you optimize your Google Business Profile, previous Geo-Grid Scans remain available for comparison. Reviewing historical scans makes it easier to measure changes in visibility and understand how your local search presence evolves over time.

### Make smarter local SEO decisions

Geo-Grid Scans provide insights that traditional rank tracking cannot. By understanding where your business is highly visible and where coverage is weaker, you can prioritize optimization efforts, refine your local SEO strategy, and make decisions based on real geographic ranking data.

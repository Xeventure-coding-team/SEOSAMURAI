## How it works

Customer reviews can sometimes disappear from your Google Business Profile without warning. Whether a review has been removed by Google, deleted by the customer, or is no longer publicly visible for another reason, losing that feedback can make it difficult to understand how your online reputation has changed over time. Rankerly's Tracked Reviews feature provides an additional layer of visibility by monitoring review history and preserving records of reviews that were previously collected.

Instead of only showing the reviews that are currently visible on your Google Business Profile, Tracked Reviews helps you maintain a historical record of customer feedback. When a review is no longer publicly available, you can still see that it previously existed, helping you identify changes that might otherwise go unnoticed. This gives businesses greater confidence when monitoring their online reputation and understanding long-term review activity.

Historical review tracking is especially valuable for businesses that receive a high volume of customer feedback or agencies managing multiple locations. Rather than wondering whether review counts have changed or why ratings have shifted, your team can identify removed or deleted reviews and maintain better visibility into the complete history of customer feedback.

Whether you're auditing review activity, investigating unexpected rating changes, or simply maintaining accurate historical records, Tracked Reviews helps you understand what has changed over time. By monitoring review visibility beyond what's currently shown on Google Business Profile, Rankerly gives businesses a more complete picture of their customer feedback history.

## Understand your complete review history

### See when reviews disappear

Reviews can occasionally become unavailable for a variety of reasons, including customer deletion or Google's moderation processes. Tracked Reviews helps you identify when a review that was previously visible is no longer publicly available, giving you greater awareness of changes that might otherwise go unnoticed. Instead of relying solely on the reviews currently displayed on your Business Profile, you can monitor how your review history evolves over time.

### Preserve historical review records

Customer feedback is valuable long after it's published. Tracked Reviews maintains a historical record of reviews your business has received, allowing you to reference past feedback even when it's no longer visible on your Google Business Profile. This provides a more complete picture of your customer interactions and helps you maintain accurate records for future reference.

### Monitor changes over time

Understanding how your online reputation changes requires more than looking at today's reviews. By tracking review history over time, you can identify patterns such as reviews being removed, changes in review volume, or shifts in your overall rating. This long-term visibility helps businesses make more informed decisions when evaluating their reputation and customer experience.

### Investigate unexpected rating changes

If your average rating or total review count changes unexpectedly, it can be difficult to determine why. Tracked Reviews provides additional context by showing historical review activity, making it easier to understand whether removed reviews may have contributed to those changes. This added transparency helps reduce uncertainty when monitoring your Business Profile.

### Better visibility for every location

Whether you're managing a single business or hundreds of locations, keeping track of review history manually isn't practical. Tracked Reviews automatically monitors review visibility for every connected location, giving your team a centralized view of historical review activity without requiring spreadsheets or manual record keeping.

### Strengthen your reputation monitoring

Effective reputation management isn't just about responding to today's reviews—it's also about understanding how your review profile changes over time. By preserving historical review information and highlighting reviews that are no longer publicly visible, Tracked Reviews provides the additional insight businesses need to monitor customer feedback with greater confidence.

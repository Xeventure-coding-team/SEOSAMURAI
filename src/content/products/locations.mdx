Connect your Google Business Profile and manage every location from a single dashboard. Whether you're managing a handful of locations or hundreds, Rankerly helps reduce manual work, improve efficiency, and gives your team a complete view of your local presence from a single platform.

## A closer look

### Add and manage locations

Every business you manage gets added into Rankerly as a location. Once added, you can see and manage them from one dashboard rather than logging into Google separately for each one. This becomes the foundation for everything else inside the platform, giving you a centralized place to oversee all of your locations and their activity. Posts, keyword tracking, health checks, analytics, and media management all live under each location you add, making it easier to stay organized and maintain consistency across your entire portfolio.

### Posting to your locations

From within Rankerly, you can create posts and publish them directly to a location's Google Business Profile. This means you don't need to open Google Business Profile itself to keep a location's posts up to date — you can do it from the same dashboard you're already using to manage everything else. For businesses and agencies managing multiple locations, this removes unnecessary account switching and helps ensure every location stays active with fresh content and updates.

### Keyword rankings per location

Rather than tracking keywords at a brand level, Rankerly checks keyword rankings for each location individually. This matters for any business with more than one location, since ranking performance for the same keyword can vary significantly from one location to another. Tracking each location separately gives you an accurate picture of how that specific listing is performing, instead of an average that can hide problems at individual locations. This makes it easier to identify underperforming locations, measure improvements over time, and make decisions based on location-specific data rather than assumptions.

### Location health monitoring

Each location has a health status you can monitor from the dashboard. This is meant to help you spot issues with a specific profile early, rather than discovering them later after they've already had an impact. By keeping an eye on the health of every location, you can quickly identify listings that may require attention and maintain a more consistent presence across all of your business locations.

### Analytics per location

Analytics are available for each location individually, so you can track how that location is performing over time rather than only seeing a combined view across your whole business. This makes it easier to understand trends, compare performance between locations, identify opportunities for improvement, and evaluate the effectiveness of your local marketing efforts with greater accuracy.

### Media management

You can upload and manage media for each of your listings directly inside Rankerly. Keeping photos and other media current on a Business Profile is an important part of maintaining an accurate and professional presence online. Rankerly makes this process simpler by allowing you to manage media from the same dashboard where you handle the rest of your location management activities, helping you keep every listing updated without needing to jump between different tools and accounts.
